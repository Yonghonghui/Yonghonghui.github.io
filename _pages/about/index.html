Hello, I'm Yanghonghui Chen (Ryan). I'm a passionate and curious engineering enthusiast with a deep interest in the intersection of hardware and intelligence, including Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs), Edge AI, ASIC design, AI Chips, and intelligent Agents.

Research Focus 🔬

Currently pursuing my studies with a focus on bridging the gap between cutting-edge technology and real-world applications, I aim to contribute to developing the next generation of intelligent systems.

Experience & Projects 💻

Throughout my academic journey, I've had the opportunity to engage in diverse research projects, from exploring neuromorphic chips to designing analog over-the-air communication systems for federated learning.

Vision & Goals 🎯

My vision is to advance the seamless integration of humans and machines. I am passionate about leveraging intelligent Agents to streamline complex workflows and deliver personalized experiences. I aim to develop more intuitive Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs) for fluid human-machine interaction, and utilize Edge AI to create a more connected and intelligent world. At the hardware level, I am focused on designing custom ASICs and AI chips to meet specialized application demands and explore neuromorphic computing to significantly reduce energy consumption. Ultimately, I strive to create technology that enhances human capability and fosters a more collaborative future between people and intelligent systems.
